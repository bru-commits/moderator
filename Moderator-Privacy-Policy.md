# Moderator — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 17, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Moderator collects, stores, and uses information when operating in Discord servers.

Moderator is a moderation bot designed to help server staff identify potential rule violations, review user reports, and take moderation actions.

By using a Discord server where Moderator is installed, you acknowledge that information may be processed as described in this policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

Moderator may collect and store the following information:

- **Discord user IDs:** Used to identify members, reporters, and moderators.
- **Server IDs:** Used to associate reports and moderation records with the relevant server.
- **Channel and message IDs:** Used to identify where reported messages originated.
- **Message content:** Content submitted through reports or flagged by automated detection.
- **Report information:** Report categories, reasons, optional reporter notes, and report status.
- **Moderation records:** Warnings, timeouts, kicks, bans, and other recorded moderation actions.
- **Moderator information:** The Discord ID of the moderator responsible for reviewing or handling a report.
- **Timestamps:** Dates and times associated with reports and moderation actions.

This information may be collected during normal bot operation, when a report is submitted, or when a potential violation is detected. Not every report or automated detection results in a moderation action.

## 3. How We Use Information

Collected information is used to:

- Identify members involved in reports and moderation cases.
- Allow moderators to investigate potential rule violations.
- Maintain warning histories and moderation records.
- Identify repeated behaviour where relevant.
- Record actions taken by moderators.
- Provide moderation statistics.
- Help server staff maintain a safer environment.

Information is not used to publicly accuse members of wrongdoing simply because they were reported or flagged.

## 4. Automated Detection and Reports

Moderator includes automated detection features that may identify potential issues, including:

- Suspicious messages or potential scams.
- Advertisements that may violate server advertising rules.
- Invalid or expired Discord invites.
- Advertisements posted in inappropriate channels.
- Messages that appear to encourage users to move conversations into direct messages.

Members may also submit reports directly to server moderators using Discord's reporting interface.

Automated detections and user-submitted reports are not proof of wrongdoing. Reports may be inaccurate, false, or ignored.

Moderators review reports and decide whether any action is appropriate. BanHammer does not automatically punish members simply because they have been reported or flagged.

## 5. Data Storage and Retention

Moderator stores its moderation data in a local SQLite database operated by the bot owner or server operator.

Information may remain stored for as long as it is considered necessary for moderation, recordkeeping, and accountability purposes.

Warning expiration does not necessarily mean that the associated database record is deleted. Expired warnings may remain stored as historical records.

Reports and moderation action records may also remain stored after they have been resolved or after a member leaves the server.

The bot does not currently provide automatic deletion of all stored information after a set period.

## 6. Data Sharing

Moderator does not sell collected information.

Stored information may be accessed by authorised server moderators or bot operators when necessary for moderation and administration.

Information is also processed through Discord's platform and API when the bot performs its functions. Discord's own Privacy Policy applies to information processed by Discord.

## 7. Data Removal Requests

Users who have questions or concerns about stored information may contact the bot operator using the email address provided below.

Requests will be reviewed individually. Some information may need to be retained for legitimate moderation, security, or recordkeeping purposes.

Submitting a request does not guarantee that all associated information will be deleted.

## 8. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to restrict access to Moderator's stored information.

However, no storage system or method of data transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Moderator's functionality or data-handling practices change.

The latest version will be made available at the location where this policy is published.
